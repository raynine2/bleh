# Tidebound: Eggfall

Cast. Steal. Hatch. Escape. Core-loop vertical slice built from Design Doc Part A, the Part A Delta, the Master Patch (Patches 1-8), the Big Update, Patch 10 (the Open Isles) and the Canon Sheet.

## What's in this slice

- **The Open Isles (Patch 10)**: the surface map is about ten times the old Nest and Shoals, all laid out in `IslesLayout.luau` (one table the builders, the server and the client scripts share).
- **Eggfall Town** (safe zone, spawn): the REF 1 fountain plaza (Tide Egg Shrine, Incubator, Tackle Shop, Nest Beacon, the Tide Slide, and Pinch, Old Harl, Sister Coralie and Gate Warden Sel) at the heart of a whole town island: Harbor Street and the docks (fish market, warehouses, the Salty Egg tavern, two piers, moored boats, a crane) out to the **Tidegate**, where the causeway to the Shoals starts; Market Row (REF 3 neon shops, food stalls round a market square, an arcade, a teahouse, string lights); Torii Hill (REF 1 snowy terraces climbed by REF 5 torii stairs to a shrine overlook, where the sky's light beam rises, and a koi pond at its foot); Hatchery Farms (crop rows, a turning windmill, red egg barns, a chicken run); South Beach (umbrellas, palms, a lifeguard tower and the ferry to the Harbor Ring); Lighthouse Point. No mobs, no heat, and you heal in town.
- **Town life (Patch 10)**: 26 villagers walk the streets, stop to chat, wave and turn to you as you pass (and drop useful hints); fishers on the piers and the koi pond cast and land fish; vendors call out from the stalls; chickens peck round the run; gulls wheel over the harbor; boats bob; the named NPCs breathe and turn to face you; butterflies flit about and fish leap out of the sea wherever you are.
- **Sunken Shoals** (biome 1, Patch 10): 22 islands in three rings east of town, each named on a floating sign you can read from far away. Ring 1, the Shallows (REF 2 boardwalks, bamboo, the lavender tree, lattice huts, Turtle Bay, Mangrove Isle, Kelp Shallows); Ring 2, the Coral Maze (checkered grass, studded paths, voxel trees and plank bridges on stilts: Shipwreck Bay, Coral Arches, the Sea Stacks with an egg on top of a climb, Smugglers' Cove and its cave, Puffer Lagoon round a glowing lagoon, Gull Rock, two atolls); Ring 3, the Outer Reef (a storm with rain and a grey sky, dark rock, dead trees and rickety rope bridges: Giant Clam Atoll, Lighthouse Isle, Maelstrom Isle beside the Maelstrom, Warden's Rock, Storm Spire, Bone Reef inside a whale's ribcage, Far Reach). 29 fishing spots (most with a jetty to stand on), 15 wild egg nests, 7 beacons, 3 Tide Caches (bank fish and heal), 14 anemone beds, 5 elite dens, the Reef Warden's patrol across Rings 2 and 3, and 8 pairs of **geysers** that throw you in a high arc (with a front flip) between far islands. A current turns you back at the edge of the charts.
- **Mobs (Patch 10)**: 20 camps across the islands and 5 in Kelp Cathedral, plus Reef Sharks in open water: Snapper Crabs (chase and snap), Spike Puffers (swell red, then blast everything in a ring), Raider Gulls (circle, screech, dive at where you stood), Reef Sharks (only bite swimmers: stay on the bridges) and Coral Golems (raise their fists, then slam a big ring). Every attack has a tell: a red "!" over the mob and, for blasts and dives, a red circle on the ground that fills before it goes off. Damage grows with your heat. **Swipe** your rod at them (left click, F, or the SWIPE button on touch): each swipe knocks them back and interrupts a wind-up, enough swipes pop them, and 30% drop a Brineworm. Health bars show once a mob is hurt or hunting you. Out of combat you slowly heal; standing in a Tide Cache's green circle heals you fast.
- **Minimap and sea chart (Patch 12)**: a round minimap in the top-right corner follows you, north up. It shows the town's coast and streets, every island with its sand shore and trees, the bridges, beacons, Tide Caches, geysers, wild eggs, the Warden's Rock, the Maelstrom and other divers. It sits in a golden ring with a compass, + and – zoom buttons and a ribbon naming where you are. Click it (or press N) for the full sea chart, framed in big anime clouds, with every island named and a legend; M hides the minimap. Entering an island pops its name and ring; leaving town warns you, coming home says so.
- **Art pass (Patch 13)**: the ground everywhere is stepped voxel strips with classic studs on top (the town island is rasterised onto a 6-stud grid; islands, terraces, the Harbor Ring and Home Nest plots too), so coasts and floors have the references' blocky, studded look; streets are studded paving with kerbs. Thick voxel grass (TideboundFoliage) grows on every lawn, meadow and checkered tile near you, in that ground's greens with the odd flower, swaying in gusts of wind and parting as you walk through. Trees are layered voxel crowns; pines are snowy cross-tiered voxel pines. Shops are detailed shopfronts (studded plinth, corner pillars, trim and cornice, framed glass display windows, a panelled door with a glowing transom, a scalloped striped awning on brackets, a framed sign with a neon border, a flag flapping on the roof, and a pot, crates or a bench out front). Cottages have stone foundations, framed glass windows with sills and flower boxes, a porch with a lantern, a stepped studded roof and a smoking chimney. Big soft snowflakes fall over the town.
- **Easier on the eyes (Patch 12)**: a lower, warmer sun, less ambient light, slight negative exposure, crisper shadows, less haze and bloom, and more contrast and colour. The snow is a soft blue-grey instead of paper white, and the stone, mint rock, sand and grass are a little deeper.
- **Kelp Cathedral (Big Update, biome 2)**: PUSH DEEPER at a Shoals beacon plays the descent and drops you into a sunken cathedral in a cavern 480 studs across: a studded nave between stone pillars and giant kelp, a rose window over the apse, 3 holy-water pools (4 fishing spots, luck x1.3, 12 new fish), 4 ruined chapels and the apse with 5 wild eggs, a whale skeleton, a Tide Cache, 2 beacons, grasping vines (Canon hazard: 40% slower), glowing jellies, fish schools and light shafts. Its guardian, the Kelp Leviathan, hits for 30 with a 0.7 s tell and only hunts divers in the Kelp. The Current at the Descent dais takes you back up. Extracting anywhere lands you back in the Nest.
- **Animation (Big Update, fixed in Patch 10)**: every player gets code-driven body motion until the animator's ids arrive (Patch 10 fixed the check that mistook Roblox's default animations for an animator's and switched every body off, finds joints by the limb they move as well as by name, and adds the whole-body fishing stance, the rod swipe and the geyser flip; R15 avatars only, see below): breathing idle with fidgets, a run that leans and strides with your speed, sprint, jump and fall, landing squash, swimming, carrying eggs, grabbing an egg, getting hit, stunned stars, going down, the extraction cheer, and the fishing arms. Voxel effects in the reference video's style: footstep puffs, landing rings, a red "!" over the bobber on a bite, cube splashes and tier-coloured bursts on a catch, guardian telegraph rings that fill before a strike, and roar bursts.
- **Cutscenes (Big Update)**: a first-join flyover, the dive off the dock, a guardian waking (it can't hurt you while it plays), the descent and the ascent, extraction (a column of light, then a tally) and a wipe. Each one can be skipped once you've arrived.
- **Diving (Master Patch 1)**: talk to Gate Warden Sel (DIVE tab) and pick DIVE SOLO (x1.15 Pearls) or DIVE WITH CREW (+5% per extra member, up to 4, invite only). Walking out of the Nest also starts a solo dive. Canon caps hold: 120 Pearls per fish, 400 per run payout. Solo and Crew dives have separate leaderboards.
- **Contested Waters (opt-in PvP)**: OFF by default, set per dive, back to OFF after it. When ON, a rival behind you can hold 1 s to snatch one carried egg; never within 40 studs of a beacon; the victim gets 5 s immunity and a speed burst. Egg nests can hold a bonus egg (x1.20). Crewmates never snatch from each other.
- **Companions**: pick 2 hatched creatures in the DIVE tab. They follow you, and each awake one carries an egg so it doesn't slow you. A Reef Warden hit knocks one out for 10 min (or use a Revive Vial).
- **Fishing minigame**: hold click or touch to push the reel bar and keep the fish inside it. Rod Control widens the bar, Resilience slows progress loss, Lure Speed shortens the bite, Luck and bait raise Rare+ odds. Perfect catches pay +20%. A fish that escapes after passing 90% drops a Lucky Scale (+25 Pearls). Patch 10 made it easier and livelier: a wider bar, a calmer fish, faster progress and slower loss, a gentle assist that leans the bar toward the fish, a gold **sweet spot** in the middle of the bar that reels 1.5x faster (and throws gold sparkles off your line), **treasure pearls** (✦) that float up on the track for +12% if you cover them, and a fish that tires for a moment at each milestone (50%, 75%, 90%). Only touches the game didn't take count as holding, so the thumbstick no longer reels. On screen: a fish shadow circles the bobber and nibbles before the bite, the bite slams the float with a burst and a "!" over your head, the reel sprays water, the fish leaps clear, the line glows red under tension, and a catch shoots a water geyser and a pillar of light in the fish's tier colour.
- **Rod animation (Master Patch 5)**: every cast plays the 10 steps of the rod table on every player's screen: windup, release (with the rod's trail, cast effect and a +4 FOV kick), landing, idle sway, the bite tell, the hook set, the struggle (the rod bends 0.6 to 0.9 with the fish's pull and the screen shakes harder for rarer fish), the snap and the landing flip into your hands. Until the animator's ids are pasted into `Animations.luau`, code poses the arms and bends the rod; the line is a spring that whips, sags and snaps taut.
- **Fish reveal (Master Patch 6)**: every catch plays a 2.8 to 4.6 s reveal: the world drains to 20% colour, the camera swings to a 45-degree side view, a silhouette climbs the tiers 0.3 s at a time, then a name card shows the biome, fish, tier, stars, mutation and weight. Epic+ adds confetti, Legendary+ a shockwave and camera pull-back, Mythic+ aurora and NPCs turning to look, and a Celestial is announced to the whole server. On 40% of catches below Celestial the climb overshoots one tier and drains back (fish only, never eggs, set by `Config.Reveal.nearMissChance`). Fish roll the egg mutation table (shown on the BAIT tab too), which multiplies Pearls inside the 120 cap. Click or tap after the silhouette to skip to the card. Nothing can hurt you while it plays.
- **Rods you can see (Master Patch 7)**: each of the 6 rods has its own model, glow, line colour, trail, cast effect and bobber. Your rod rides on your back everywhere and moves to your hand when you cast; your Rod Workshop rack shows every rod you own. Sound cues are listed in `Audio.luau` with empty ids, ready for the sound designer.
- **Rod durability (Master Patch 2)**: 100 per rod, -1 per cast, -10 when a fish snaps the line. A broken rod fishes like a Drift Rod until Old Harl repairs it for Pearls (RODS tab).
- **Heat 0-100**: +2 per fish, +8 per steal, -1 per 3s while lying low. 25 Warden patrols, 50 riptide (a 6 s pull toward open water every 20 s), 75 Warden locks on, 100 every beacon closes for 30s (10 s grace if you're within 10 studs of one).
- **Risk (Master Patch 2)**: 100 health, healed between dives. The Reef Warden hits for 25, knocks your newest egg back to its shrine and knocks out a companion. Fish are cargo until you bank them at the Tide Cache; a wipe loses 100% of unbanked cargo. Wiping at heat 75+ starts your next dive at heat 15 until you rest 3 min at your Home Nest. Each carried egg costs 2 WalkSpeed; Shift (or SPRINT on touch) adds 6. Mending Vials heal 25 (Nim, Pearls only, 3 vials per dive).
- **Reef Warden (Master Patch 3)**: Phase 1 on your 3rd stolen egg (it guards the exit), Phase 2 at heat 75 (it chases; 6 s out of sight drops it back), Phase 3 at heat 100 (faster). Every strike has a 0.6 s wind-up. Escaping Phase 3 earns a Reef Core.
- **Shoals threats (Master Patch 3)**: Barnacle Brutes rattle, then charge (a hit stuns 1.5 s); Snapjaw Morays lunge from glowing holes (10 damage). Both can be looted for bait while dazed. Anemone beds sting for 10 and slow you. Glass Calm weather every 15 min: bites 30% faster, no riptide, first catch +3 Brineworm.
- **Extraction and the Greed Window (Master Patch 2)**: hold 3 s at a beacon, then choose within 10 s: EXTRACT NOW, or PUSH DEEPER for +40% cargo, +25 heat and an angrier Warden, then extract at a different beacon. No answer extracts. Payout is 50 + 30 per egg (cap 400), plus fish Pearls and 5-25 Run Embers. Clean Extraction streak adds up to +3 Embers.
- **Harbor Ring hub (Delta Section 8)**: a ring town around a swimmable lagoon south of the Nest (reach it by the Tide Slide). Lagoon pier fishing (no Heat, half Pearls, banked at once), Daily Quest Board (Quill), Season Track board (Captain Brask), Leaderboard Hall (Archivist Ophel), Bait Shop (Nim), Trading Post (Tradesman Kobb), Social Plaza with stage, photo spot and heist board.
- **Home Nest plots (Delta Section 7)**: 8 plots of 120 x 120 around the Harbor Ring, one per player. Hatch Chamber, Aquarium Hall, Rod Workshop, Trophy Room and Prestige Altar (the 5 rooms), plus Creature Pen, Nest Vault and Visitor Deck outdoors. Zone unlocks and 7 upgrade tracks (incubator slots, aquarium capacity, Tank Filter, treadmill, pen size, trophy pedestals, vault capacity) on the HOME tab. Creatures swim in the tank, roam the pen, stand on trophy pedestals and on the Showcase Plinth. Visitors (Public / Friends / Private) appear in the guestbook and can Admire the showcase for +1 Egg Shard to the owner (10 per day). Nothing in a base can be stolen.
- **Hatch scene (Patch 11)**: hatching plays a cutscene in the reference short's style. A big voxel Tide Egg with a "?" drops in front of you while a slot row of creature coins spins across the top of the screen and lands on yours. The egg shakes three times, harder each time, cracking and growing as the camera pushes in and shakes. Then it swells huge, glowing in the rarity's colour as rings pulse out from it, and bursts in a white flash with shell chunks, a storm of yellow sparkles, lightning and shock rings. The creature spins in the light with rays behind it and its rarity and name in big pixel letters; Epic and up add confetti. Click to continue, and the summary card follows. A pixel odds card (every tier's chance) floats over the Tide Egg Shrine.
- **Tide Egg gacha**: canon 7-tier odds, independent mutation roll, pity at 25/90/300/1200 per egg type, last-10 pull history, confirm modal with odds before every purchase. Hatch one egg per incubator slot at once. Shards 1 per new creature, 10 per duplicate.
- **Economy (Delta Section 11)**: aquarium income (1% of value per minute for the earning creatures, plus filter, trophy and fed bonuses), offline income at 50% for up to 8 h, duplicate sales at 50% value, 3 daily quests (300-800 Pearls), run payout and Greed pushes, Lucky Scales and pier fishing. Sinks: rods, repairs, bait, vials, eggs, zone unlocks and upgrades.
- **Feel (Delta Section 10)**: camera FOV per situation (70 explore, 65 fishing, 80 chase, 55 hatch), screen-edge tint by Heat, hit shake and flash, extraction flash, big call-outs for every strike tell and Warden phase. `src/shared/Animations.luau` lists every animation from Table A with an empty id: paste the animator's asset ids there and they play on their triggers.
- **Saves** via DataStore (`TideboundEggfall_v1`). Run state is never saved.

Robux sells nothing in this slice; eggs, odds, pity, upgrades and power are all reachable by free play.

**Studio settings for Patch 10**: set Game Settings > Avatar > Avatar Type to **R15** (the custom idle, run, fishing and swipe animations pose R15 joints; on an R6 avatar they stay off and the output says so), and turn on **Workspace.StreamingEnabled**, since the map is about ten times bigger.
