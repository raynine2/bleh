# Tidebound: Eggfall

Cast. Steal. Hatch. Escape. Core-loop vertical slice built from Design Doc Part A, the Part A Delta, the Master Patch (Patches 1-8), the Big Update, Patch 10 (the Open Isles) and the Canon Sheet.

## Working together (Team Create + Rojo): read this first

Rojo makes Studio match the files in `src/`. When it syncs, **the files win**: any script in a Rojo folder that was
edited only in Studio is overwritten. Rojo manages exactly four places:

| Studio | Files |
| --- | --- |
| `ReplicatedStorage.Tidebound` | `src/shared` |
| `ServerScriptService.Tidebound` | `src/server` |
| `StarterPlayer.StarterPlayerScripts` | `src/client` |
| `ReplicatedFirst` | `src/first` (the loading screen) |

All four keep anything that isn't in the files (`$ignoreUnknownInstances`), so Rojo never deletes a new script made
in Studio. But **edits to the scripts that come from `src/` are always replaced** on the next sync.

1. **Change code in the files, never in Studio.** Clone the repo, edit in `src/` (Claude Code, VS Code...), commit,
   push. Everyone else pulls. That includes Claude working through a Studio plugin: have it edit the files instead.
2. **Commit before you pull:** `git add -A`, `git commit -m "..."`, `git pull`. Then a pull merges, never hides.
3. **One person runs Rojo** in the shared Studio, and only after checking nobody has unsaved script edits there.
4. **Studio-only work is fine outside those four places** (Workspace builds, StarterGui, your own folders). Rojo
   never touches them; Team Create saves them with the place. A script you make in Studio inside a Rojo folder
   survives, but save it into `src/` and commit it soon, so it's in git too.
5. When a script is deleted from `src/`, delete it in Studio by hand too (Rojo keeps unknown instances now).

## What's in this slice

- **The Open Isles (Patch 10)**: the surface map is about ten times the old Nest and Shoals, all laid out in `IslesLayout.luau` (one table the builders, the server and the client scripts share).
- **Eggfall Town** (safe zone, spawn): the REF 1 fountain plaza (Tide Egg Shrine, Incubator, Tackle Shop, Nest Beacon, the Tide Slide, and Pinch, Old Harl, Sister Coralie and Gate Warden Sel) at the heart of a whole town island: Harbor Street and the docks (fish market, warehouses, the Salty Egg tavern, two piers, moored boats, a crane) out to the **Tidegate**, where the causeway to the Shoals starts; Market Row (REF 3 neon shops, food stalls round a market square, an arcade, a teahouse, string lights); Torii Hill (REF 1 snowy terraces climbed by REF 5 torii stairs to a shrine overlook, where the sky's light beam rises, and a koi pond at its foot); Hatchery Farms (crop rows, a turning windmill, red egg barns, a chicken run); South Beach (umbrellas, palms, a lifeguard tower and the ferry to the Harbor Ring); Lighthouse Point. No mobs, no heat, and you heal in town.
- **Town life (Patch 10)**: 26 villagers walk the streets, stop to chat, wave and turn to you as you pass (and drop useful hints); fishers on the piers and the koi pond cast and land fish; vendors call out from the stalls; chickens peck round the run; gulls wheel over the harbor; boats bob; the named NPCs breathe and turn to face you; butterflies flit about and fish leap out of the sea wherever you are.
- **Sunken Shoals** (biome 1, Patch 10): 22 islands in three rings east of town, each named on a floating sign you can read from far away. Ring 1, the Shallows (REF 2 boardwalks, bamboo, the lavender tree, lattice huts, Turtle Bay, Mangrove Isle, Kelp Shallows); Ring 2, the Coral Maze (checkered grass, studded paths, voxel trees and plank bridges on stilts: Shipwreck Bay, Coral Arches, the Sea Stacks with an egg on top of a climb, Smugglers' Cove and its cave, Puffer Lagoon round a glowing lagoon, Gull Rock, two atolls); Ring 3, the Outer Reef (a storm with rain and a grey sky, dark rock, dead trees and rickety rope bridges: Giant Clam Atoll, Lighthouse Isle, Maelstrom Isle beside the Maelstrom, Warden's Rock, Storm Spire, Bone Reef inside a whale's ribcage, Far Reach). 29 fishing spots (most with a jetty to stand on), 15 wild egg nests, 7 beacons, 3 Tide Caches (bank fish and heal), 14 anemone beds, 5 elite dens, the Reef Warden's patrol across Rings 2 and 3, and 8 pairs of **geysers** that throw you in a high arc (with a front flip) between far islands. A current turns you back at the edge of the charts.
- **Mobs (Patch 10)**: 20 camps across the islands and 5 in Kelp Cathedral, plus Reef Sharks in open water: Snapper Crabs (chase and snap), Spike Puffers (swell red, then blast everything in a ring), Raider Gulls (circle, screech, dive at where you stood), Reef Sharks (only bite swimmers: stay on the bridges) and Coral Golems (raise their fists, then slam a big ring). Every attack has a tell: a red "!" over the mob and, for blasts and dives, a red circle on the ground that fills before it goes off. Damage grows with your heat. **Swipe** your rod at them (left click, F, or the SWIPE button on touch): each swipe knocks them back and interrupts a wind-up, enough swipes pop them, and 30% drop a Brineworm. Health bars show once a mob is hurt or hunting you. Out of combat you slowly heal; standing in a Tide Cache's green circle heals you fast.
- **Minimap and sea chart (Patch 12)**: a round minimap in the top-right corner follows you, north up. It shows the town's coast and streets, every island with its sand shore and trees, the bridges, beacons, Tide Caches, geysers, wild eggs, the Warden's Rock, the Maelstrom and other divers. It sits in a golden ring with a compass, + and – zoom buttons and a ribbon naming where you are. Click it (or press N) for the full sea chart, framed in big anime clouds, with every island named and a legend; M hides the minimap. Entering an island pops its name and ring; leaving town warns you, coming home says so.

- **Feel (Delta Section 10)**: camera FOV per situation (70 explore, 65 fishing, 80 chase, 55 hatch), screen-edge tint by Heat, hit shake and flash, extraction flash, big call-outs for every strike tell and Warden phase. `src/shared/Animations.luau` lists every animation from Table A with an empty id: paste the animator's asset ids there and they play on their triggers.
- **Saves** via DataStore (`TideboundEggfall_v1`). Run state is never saved.

Robux sells nothing in this slice; eggs, odds, pity, upgrades and power are all reachable by free play.
