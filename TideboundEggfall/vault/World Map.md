# World Map

The whole map plan is one table, `IslesLayout.luau` (Patch 10), that builders, server and client all read.

- [[Eggfall Town]]: the safe spawn, with the plaza in the middle
- [[Harbor Ring]]: the ring town round a lagoon, reached by the Tide Slide
- [[Home Nest]]: 8 player plots round the Harbor Ring
- [[Sunken Shoals]]: 22 islands in three rings east of town
- [[Kelp Cathedral]]: biome 2, under the sea
- [[Horizon]]: scenery that hides the edge of the world

Shown on the [[Minimap]]. Built by the [[Builders]].
