# Visual Effects

- **`VfxLibrary` + `TideboundVfx`:** every gameplay effect from the GDD (egg auras in rarity colours, the extraction beam, guardian roars, the PUSH DEEPER rift, sonar pulses, water impacts, bubble trails, dash flares), with clarity rules: at most 500 particles per action, at most 3 screen flashes a second (20% with Reduce Flashing), effects fading out past 150 studs, and a VFX Quality setting.
- **`WaterFx`:** realistic splashes for casts and catches (particle crowns, droplets, mist, foam), sparkling in the fish's tier colour ([[Fishing]]).
- **`RodFx`:** rod-flavoured bursts with camera punches and screen shake, in each rod's colours ([[Rod Combo]], [[Stances]]).
- **`Fx`:** voxel one-shots (cube bursts, gold bolts, shock rings, "!" alerts).
