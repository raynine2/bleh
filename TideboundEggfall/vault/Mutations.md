# Mutations

Every hatched creature also rolls a mutation, separately from its tier (`Config.Mutations`). The mutation multiplies its Pearl value.

| Mutation | Chance | Value |
|---|---|---|
| None | 70% | x1 |
| Brine | 15% | x1.25 |
| Storm | 9% | x1.6 |
| Aurora | 4% | x2.2 |
| Abyssal | 1.5% | x3.5 |
| Prismatic | 0.5% | x6 |

Applies to creatures from [[Tide Eggs]] and creature prizes from the [[Tide Chest]]. Not to dashes.
