# Overview

**Tidebound: Eggfall** is a Roblox extraction game with a gacha. You dive from the safe town into the [[Sunken Shoals]], fish, steal eggs and fight [[Mobs]] while your [[Heat]] rises, then get out through a beacon ([[Extraction and Greed]]). Back home you hatch eggs ([[Tide Eggs]]), open [[Dash Capsules]], and grow your [[Home Nest]].

- **Look:** chunky voxel blocks with classic studs; the town is the snowy reference town (see [[Eggfall Town]]). Fishing plays like *Fisch* ([[Fishing]]).
- **Fair play:** Robux only buys Tideglass for cosmetics ([[LiveOps]]). Eggs, odds, pity, speed and power are never for sale. The odds you see are the odds the server rolls ([[Gacha]]).
- **Built with:** Rojo; the code lives in `TideboundEggfall/src` ([[Scripts]], [[Rojo Workflow]]).
