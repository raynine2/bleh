# Underwater

`TideboundUnderwater` works out when the camera is under the sea and blends in a deep-blue colour grade, a slight blur, dense blue fog, deep-blue light and a soft vignette behind the HUD, smoothly (no flicker at the surface). Other scripts read it through `ClientBus` ([[Audio]] muffles, [[Biomes]] hands over the fog). See [[Swimming and Riding]].
