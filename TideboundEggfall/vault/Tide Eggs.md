# Tide Eggs

The main gacha. **300 Pearls** at the shrine, or steal eggs for free on a dive ([[Core Loop]]).

- **Hatch** at the incubator or the Tide Egg shrine on the plaza ([[Eggfall Town]]), or your Hatch Chamber ([[Home Nest]]). You hatch one egg per incubator slot at once.
- **Odds:** [[Rarity Tiers]] plus a [[Mutations|mutation]] roll. Pity counter: `TideEgg` ([[Pity]]).
- **Result:** a creature goes into your aquarium ([[Home Nest]]) and can become a [[Companions|companion]].
- Plays the egg version of the [[Hatch Scene]].
