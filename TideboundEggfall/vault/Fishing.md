# Fishing

Cast at glowing spots. Hold click or touch to push the reel bar and keep the fish inside it.
- **Rod stats** ([[Rods]]): Control widens the bar, Resilience slows progress loss, Lure Speed shortens the wait, Luck raises Rare+ odds.
- **Bait** multiplies luck: Brineworm x1.1 (15 Pearls), Glowmite x1.25 (40), Stormfin x1.45, Abyssal Squid x1.7, Aurora Krill x2, Prismatic Roe x2.5. Shop bait from [[Nim]], the rest drops in the wild.
- Fish are cargo until you bank them at a Tide Cache. Each fish adds [[Heat]].
- Every catch plays a reveal; the rod animates through `TideboundRod`.
