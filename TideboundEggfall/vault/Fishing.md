# Fishing

Fishing plays like *Fisch* (12 and 13 Oct 2026, `FischUI`, `TideboundFischCast`, `TideboundClient`):

1. **Power cast:** in the fishing stance ([[Stances]]), hold click (or touch, E, R2) and let go. A slim bar bounces beside you; the higher you stop it, the better the word over your head (Perfect!, Amazing!!, Great!, Good, Okay) and the faster you reel, up to +30%. You can cast at a spot or into **any water** in front of you.
2. **SHAKE:** while you wait for the bite, numbered circles pop up *osu!*-style with rings closing in; hit each as its ring meets it. Rarer fish bring more, faster circles.
3. **Reel:** hold to keep the fish inside the bar. Rare fish and up fight back mid-reel with a burst of circles.

- **Rod stats** ([[Rods]]): Control widens the bar, Resilience slows progress loss, Lure Speed shortens the wait, Luck raises Rare+ odds.
- **Bait** multiplies luck: Brineworm x1.1 (15 Pearls), Glowmite x1.25 (40), Stormfin x1.45, Abyssal Squid x1.7, Aurora Krill x2, Prismatic Roe x2.5. Shop bait from [[Nim]].
- Fish are cargo until you bank them at a Tide Cache. Each fish adds [[Heat]].
- Splashes and catches are realistic particle water (`WaterFx`, [[Visual Effects]]).
- More on top: Surges, roaming rare fish and rod roles in [[Fishing Overhaul]].
