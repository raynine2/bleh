# Home Nest

8 plots of 120 x 120 round the [[Harbor Ring]], one per player (`BaseService.luau`, `PlotLayout.luau`).

- **Rooms:** Hatch Chamber ([[Tide Eggs]]), Aquarium Hall, Rod Workshop ([[Rods]]), Trophy Room, Prestige Altar.
- **Outdoor:** Creature Pen (feed trough, treadmill for WalkSpeed), Nest Vault, Visitor Deck with a guestbook, Showcase Plinth.
- Aquarium creatures earn Pearls ([[Economy]]). Nothing banked here can be stolen.
- A bigger base system with themes and more rooms is drafted in [[Base Builder]] but not hooked up yet.
