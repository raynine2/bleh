# Rarity Tiers

Every gacha uses these seven tiers and rates (`Config.Tiers`). The server rolls them in `Gacha.rollTier`.

| # | Tier | Rate | Pearl value | Creatures |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Common | 58% | 50 | Tide Sprite, Barnacle Pup |
| 2 | Uncommon | 24% | 120 | Kelp Crawler, Shellback Newt |
| 3 | Rare | 12% | 300 | Storm Eel, Coral Drake |
| 4 | Epic | 4.5% | 800 | Abyss Angler, Rift Serpent |
| 5 | Legendary | 1.2% | 2500 | Tempest Wyrm, Leviathan Calf |
| 6 | Mythic | 0.25% | 8000 | Aurora Kraken, Deep Sovereign |
| 7 | Celestial | 0.05% | 30000 | Tidebound God, Prismatic Leviathan |

Used by [[Tide Eggs]], [[Dash Capsules]] and [[Tide Chest]]; every dash in [[Dashes]] and every creature has one. See also [[Pity]] and [[Mutations]].
