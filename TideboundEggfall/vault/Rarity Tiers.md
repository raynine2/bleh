# Rarity Tiers

Every gacha uses these seven tiers and rates (`Config.Tiers`). The server rolls them in `Gacha.rollTier`.

| # | Tier | Rate | Pearl value | Creatures |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Common | 58% | 50 | Goldfish, Guppy, Tetra, Molly, Platy |
| 2 | Uncommon | 24% | 120 | Angelfish, Betta, Gourami, Swordtail, Rainbowfish |
| 3 | Rare | 12% | 300 | Clownfish, Tang, Discus, Oscar, Arowana |
| 4 | Epic | 4.5% | 800 | Lionfish, Pufferfish, MandarinFish, MoorishIdol, Seahorse |
| 5 | Legendary | 1.2% | 2500 | LuffyFish, PikachuFish, GokuFish, MarioFish, SonicFish |
| 6 | Mythic | 0.25% | 8000 | NarutoFish, SaitamaFish, TotoroFish |
| 7 | Celestial | 0.05% | 30000 | KirbyFish, DeadpoolFish |

Used by [[Tide Eggs]] and [[Dash Capsules]]; every dash in [[Dashes]] and every creature has one. See also [[Pity]] and [[Mutations]].

Creatures are voxel fish models (`ReplicatedStorage.VoxelFish`, shown with `FishView` and `FishThumbs`).

> ⚠️ The Legendary to Celestial fish are named after well-known characters (Luffy, Pikachu, Goku, Mario, Sonic, Naruto, Saitama, Totoro, Kirby, Deadpool). Roblox can take down games that use other companies' characters, so give them original names before you publish.
