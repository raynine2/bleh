# Economy

- **Pearls** come from fishing, extraction and aquarium income: 1% of a creature's value per minute for earning creatures, plus bonuses; offline income at 50% for up to 8 h.
- **Spend** on [[Tide Eggs]], [[Dash Capsules]], [[Rods]] and [[Home Nest]] upgrades.
- **Shards** come from gacha pulls: 1 for new, 10 for a duplicate ([[Gacha]]).
- 3 daily quests from [[Quill]]. Robux buys nothing.
