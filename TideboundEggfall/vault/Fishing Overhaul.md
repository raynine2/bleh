# Fishing Overhaul

12 Oct 2026, copied from Fisch's "Best Money Spots" video (`FishingPlus`, `FishingPlusService`, `TideboundFishingPlus`). It never changes the canon values (rod stats, bait luck, tier odds, mutation odds, the Pearl caps of 120 per fish and 400 per run); it only leans the rarity roll.

- **Surges:** each biome's timed event, like Fisch's Brine Storm. Disturbance builds from every catch in the biome (and slowly on its own); at 100 a Surge spawns for 5 minutes, then rests 7. Fishing inside one boosts luck. Only built biomes get one ([[Biomes]]).
- **Roaming rare fish:** glowing fish you can see swimming near the spots, with their name in their tier's colour. Cast at the spot they circle to hook that fish. More come out at night.
- **Conditions:** rod x bait x biome x night x Surge all multiply, and the cast shows the full sum.
- **Rod roles:** Drift *Steady*; Coral *Tidecaller* (double Disturbance); Stormline *Static Hook* (bites 30% faster in a Surge); Abyssal *Deep Lure* (hooks roamers from further away); Riftcaster *Rift Echo* (Surge luck x1.8); Celestial *Starfall* (bigger head start from a MAX cast). See [[Rods]].
- **MAX cast:** a power bar over your head while the line flies; stop it at the top for "MAX!" and a head start on the reel.
- **Run summary:** after extraction, big green Pearls and Pearls per minute ([[Extraction and Greed]]).
