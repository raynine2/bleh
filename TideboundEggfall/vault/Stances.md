# Stances

13 Oct 2026 (`TideboundStance`). Press **X** (gamepad Y, or tap the pill at the bottom of the screen) to swap between:
- **Fishing stance:** a click at the water charges a power cast ([[Fishing]]).
- **Fighting stance:** a click swings your rod ([[Rod Combo]]); the rod stays in your hand in a low guard.

The swap twirls the rod, punches the camera and bursts in your rod's colours (`RodFx`). On screen it's one small FISH | FIGHT pill to the right of the [[Rod Abilities|rod ability]] pill; it hides while you fish.
