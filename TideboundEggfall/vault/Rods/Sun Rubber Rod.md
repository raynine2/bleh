# Sun Rubber Rod

Part of [[Rods]].

- **How to get:** Unlocked through [[Rod Trials]]: 60 mobs of any kind and 8 golems.
- **Stats:** control 1.6, luck 2.1, lure speed 2, resilience 2.2. Works in 5 biomes.

## Ability: Sun God Bounce
Drums, cartoon clouds and white flames, then a giant stretching rubber fist.

- **Key:** R (Y on a gamepad, SKILL on touch). **Cooldown:** 14 s.
- This is an **ultimate**: a short cinematic (camera takes over, letterbox bars, the name across the screen) before it lands.
- See [[Rod Abilities]] for how abilities work.
