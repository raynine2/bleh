# Coral Rod

Part of [[Rods]].

- **How to get:** Buy from [[Old Harl]] for 2,500 Pearls.
- **Stats:** control 1.1, luck 1.2, lure speed 1.15, resilience 1.15. Works in 3 biomes.

## Ability: Coral Bloom
Stab the rod down; coral spikes erupt in a ring.

- **Key:** R (Y on a gamepad, SKILL on touch). **Cooldown:** 7 s.
