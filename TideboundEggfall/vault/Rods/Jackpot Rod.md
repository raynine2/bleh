# Jackpot Rod

Part of [[Rods]].

- **How to get:** Unlocked through [[Rod Trials]]: 30 crabs and 15 puffers. *Beat enough crabs and puffers and your luck turns.*
- **Stats:** control 1.5, luck 2.4, lure speed 1.6, resilience 1.6. Works in 4 biomes.

## Ability: Jackpot Rush
The reels spin; on a jackpot you're healed and untouchable for a while.

- **Key:** R (Y on a gamepad, SKILL on touch). **Cooldown:** 15 s.
- This is an **ultimate**: a short cinematic (camera takes over, letterbox bars, the name across the screen) before it lands.
- See [[Rod Abilities]] for how abilities work.
