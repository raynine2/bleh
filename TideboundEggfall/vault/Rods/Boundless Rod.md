# Boundless Rod

Part of [[Rods]].

- **How to get:** Unlocked through [[Rod Trials]]: 150 mobs of any kind, 15 golems, and the other four trial rods unlocked.
- **Stats:** control 2.3, luck 2.8, lure speed 2.4, resilience 2.3. Works in 5 biomes.

## Ability: Boundless Void
A dome of endless void; red and blue merge into a violet core that's fired.

- **Key:** R (Y on a gamepad, SKILL on touch). **Cooldown:** 25 s.
- This is an **ultimate**: a short cinematic (camera takes over, letterbox bars, the name across the screen) before it lands.
- See [[Rod Abilities]] for how abilities work.
