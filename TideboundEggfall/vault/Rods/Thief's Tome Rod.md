# Thief's Tome Rod

Part of [[Rods]].

- **How to get:** Unlocked through [[Rod Trials]]: 5 golems and 8 sharks. *Take on the big ones.*
- **Stats:** control 1.9, luck 2, lure speed 1.8, resilience 1.8. Works in 5 biomes.

## Ability: Stolen Page
The book flips to a stolen page and casts another rod's ability.

- **Key:** R (Y on a gamepad, SKILL on touch). **Cooldown:** 12 s.
- This is an **ultimate**: a short cinematic (camera takes over, letterbox bars, the name across the screen) before it lands.
- See [[Rod Abilities]] for how abilities work.
