# Riftcaster

Part of [[Rods]].

- **How to get:** Buy from [[Old Harl]] for 120,000 Pearls.
- **Stats:** control 1.7, luck 2.1, lure speed 1.9, resilience 1.85. Works in 5 biomes.

## Ability: Rift Slash
Three slashes tear space open, then it shatters.

- **Key:** R (Y on a gamepad, SKILL on touch). **Cooldown:** 9 s.
