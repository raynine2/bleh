# Crimson Crow Rod

Part of [[Rods]].

- **How to get:** Unlocked through [[Rod Trials]]: 25 gulls and 10 puffers. *Hunt the gulls that circle the islands.*
- **Stats:** control 1.8, luck 1.9, lure speed 1.7, resilience 1.6. Works in 4 biomes.

## Ability: Crimson Illusion
A crimson spiral eye opens; a storm of crows; everything nearby is frozen.

- **Key:** R (Y on a gamepad, SKILL on touch). **Cooldown:** 14 s.
- This is an **ultimate**: a short cinematic (camera takes over, letterbox bars, the name across the screen) before it lands.
- See [[Rod Abilities]] for how abilities work.
