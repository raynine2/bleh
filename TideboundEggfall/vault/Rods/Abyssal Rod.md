# Abyssal Rod

Part of [[Rods]].

- **How to get:** Buy from [[Old Harl]] for 40,000 Pearls.
- **Stats:** control 1.45, luck 1.75, lure speed 1.6, resilience 1.55. Works in 4 biomes.

## Ability: Abyss Maw
A vortex drags mobs in, then shadow jaws snap shut.

- **Key:** R (Y on a gamepad, SKILL on touch). **Cooldown:** 9 s.
- This is a quick ability with a caption.
