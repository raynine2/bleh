# Rod Combo

Click (or F, or the SWIPE button) for a 3-hit combo (Patch 17):
1. **Bonk:** an overhead chop with a white arc and a cartoon bonk.
2. **Whirl:** a one-leg spin with a rainbow ring.
3. **Fish slap:** a giant fish pops into your hands and slams down; stars ring.

Knocks back [[Mobs]]; enough hits pop them for bait. Settings in `Config.Swipe` (range 9, 0.32 s between swings). See also [[Rod Abilities]] and [[Dashes]].
