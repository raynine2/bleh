# Swimming and Riding

12 Oct 2026, "Swimming + Fish Riding" (`WaterMoves`, `TideboundRide`, `RideService`).

- **Swimming:** tread water when still, front crawl on the move, butterfly while sprinting, a dolphin kick under the surface. Water flicks off each stroke. See [[Underwater]].
- **Riding:** press **G** (gamepad L1, or RIDE on touch) in the sea and your first [[Companions|companion]] fish pops up under you (or a chunky orange loaner fish). Glide with the movement keys, Shift for turbo, Space to leap with a flip, G again or reaching a beach to hop off. Each ride rolls a goofy style: Surf, Cowboy, Superman or Noodle. Foam wakes, spray, ripples and a "YEEHAW!".
- Free fun movement: no Robux, no stats. Fishing, a cutscene or going down ends the ride.
