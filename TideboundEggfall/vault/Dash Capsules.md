# Dash Capsules

The dash gacha (Patch 20). **400 Pearls** each, at the Dash Capsule machine on the plaza ([[Eggfall Town]]) or in the DASHES tab ([[Menu Tabs]]).

- Each capsule gives a dash move of the rolled rarity ([[Dashes]]). If no dash exists at that rarity yet, it gives one from the nearest rarity below.
- **Equip** up to 3 dashes; **Q** only rolls equipped ones. A new dash goes straight into a free slot. You always keep at least one. Everyone starts with Zoomies.
- Same rates as eggs ([[Rarity Tiers]]); pity counter `DashCapsule` ([[Pity]]).
- Plays the [[Hatch Scene]] with a glassy capsule instead of an egg, and a spinning dash medallion as the reveal.
