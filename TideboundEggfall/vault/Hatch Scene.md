# Hatch Scene

The cutscene every gacha plays (`HatchScene.luau`, Patch 11), in the style of the reference short:
1. The egg (or capsule, or chest) drops in front of you; a slot row of coins spins across the top of the screen.
2. It shakes three times, cracking and growing; the camera pushes in.
3. It swells and glows in the rarity's colour while rings pulse out.
4. It bursts (or, for the [[Tide Chest]], the lid flips open): flash, chunks, sparkles, lightning, shock rings.
5. The prize spins in the light with its rarity and name in big pixel letters.

The wheel never shows your prize until it opens. Click to skip once the reveal shows. Used by [[Tide Eggs]], [[Dash Capsules]] and [[Tide Chest]].
