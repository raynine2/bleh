# Tide Chest

The chest gacha (Patch 23), copied from the chest gacha in *Catch a Brainrot*. **600 Pearls**, opened on the spot.

- **Where:** the treasure chest in front of the Tide Lab ([[Eggfall Town]]), or the CHEST tab ([[Menu Tabs]]).
- **What's inside:** rolls a rarity ([[Rarity Tiers]]), then either a **creature** of that rarity (goes in your aquarium, with a [[Mutations|mutation]] roll) or, 50% of the time, a **dash** ([[Dashes]]).
- **Pity:** its own counter, `TideChest` ([[Pity]]).
- **Cutscene:** the [[Hatch Scene]] with a wooden treasure chest: it drops, shakes and glows while the icon row spins (a mix of creature and dash icons), then the lid flips open and the prize rises out.
- **Settings:** `Config.Chest = { pearlCost = 600, dashChance = 0.5 }`. Can't be opened on a dive.
