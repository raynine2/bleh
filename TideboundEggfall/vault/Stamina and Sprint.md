# Stamina and Sprint

Patch 18. Sprint (Shift / SPRINT) eases in and out, widens the view a little and drains stamina. It refills after a short rest, faster standing still. At empty you're winded for a moment.

[[Dashes]] spend stamina too. The value is shared on [[MotionBus]] (`MotionBus.stamina`) so `TideboundClient` and `TideboundDash` agree.
