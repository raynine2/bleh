# Minimap

Round minimap in the top-right corner (`TideboundMap`), north up, with a golden compass ring and zoom buttons: coast, streets, islands, bridges, beacons, Tide Caches, geysers, wild eggs and other divers. Click it (or press **N**) for the full sea chart framed in anime clouds; **M** hides it. Drawn from `IslesLayout` ([[World Map]]).
