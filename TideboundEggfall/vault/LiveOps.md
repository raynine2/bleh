# LiveOps

Retention systems (`LiveOps.luau` server, `LiveOpsData.luau` tables, `TideboundMenus` windows). The server checks every claim and purchase; the menus only display.

- **Daily quests** and **weekly goals**.
- **Seasonal events.**
- **Season pass:** *Season 1: Rising Tide* (1 Oct to 31 Dec 2026), 1000 XP per tier. The premium pass isn't set up yet.
- **Collection goals.**
- **Shop:** cosmetics (trails and titles) for Pearls or Tideglass; supplies (bait and vials). **Tideglass packs for Robux** (49, 249 or 499 R\$; product ids still 0). Tideglass and Robux only buy cosmetics.
- **Global leaderboards:** Coins and Eggs, written every 5 minutes, top 100.
- **Settings** saved per player, and a MemoryStore mirror of [[Pity]].

Windows: QUESTS, season PASS, SHOP, BOARDS, egg ODDS and SETTINGS, plus a launcher column beside the main menu ([[Menu Tabs]]). The numbers are placeholders you can change in `LiveOpsData.luau`.
