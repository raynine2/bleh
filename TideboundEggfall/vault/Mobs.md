# Mobs

Wild mobs (`Mobs.luau`, drawn by `TideboundMobs`): **Snapper Crabs** (chase and snap), **Spike Puffers** (swell, then blast a ring), **Raider Gulls** (circle and dive), **Coral Golems**, and **Reef Sharks** that only bite swimmers. Every attack is telegraphed with a red "!" or a ring on the ground; damage grows with [[Heat]].

- **Elites** (`Threats.luau`): Barnacle Brutes and Snapjaw Morays. Dodged, not killed; loot them for bait while they recover.
- **Guardians:** the Reef Warden ([[Sunken Shoals]]) and the Kelp Leviathan ([[Kelp Cathedral]]).
- Beat mobs with the [[Rod Combo]] and [[Rod Abilities]]. Kills count toward [[Rod Trials]].
