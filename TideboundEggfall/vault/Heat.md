# Heat

0 to 100 on a dive (`Config.Heat`), shown as a slim pill at the top of the screen.
- **+2** per fish, **+8** per stolen egg, **-1** every 3 s lying low.
- **25:** the Reef Warden patrols. **50:** riptide pulls. **75:** the Warden locks on. **100:** every beacon closes for 30 s.

Mob damage also grows with heat ([[Mobs]]). Lower it before you [[Extraction and Greed|extract]]. Part of the [[Core Loop]].
