# Rod Trials

Five trial rods are unlocked by defeating [[Mobs]] (Patch 21). Progress counts every mob you beat, per kind (`data.mobKills`). Track it in the TRIALS tab ([[Menu Tabs]]). The goals live in `Config.RodTrials` and can be changed later.

| # | Rod | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[Crimson Crow Rod]] | 25 gulls and 10 puffers. *Hunt the gulls that circle the islands.* |
| 2 | [[Jackpot Rod]] | 30 crabs and 15 puffers. *Beat enough crabs and puffers and your luck turns.* |
| 3 | [[Thief's Tome Rod]] | 5 golems and 8 sharks. *Take on the big ones.* |
| 4 | [[Sun Rubber Rod]] | 60 mobs of any kind and 8 golems. |
| 5 | [[Boundless Rod]] | 150 mobs of any kind, 15 golems, and the other four trial rods unlocked. |

Each trial rod has an ultimate ([[Rod Abilities]]).
