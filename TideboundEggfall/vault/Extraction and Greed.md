# Extraction and Greed

Hold 3 s at a beacon (a Tide Gate), then choose within 10 s:
- **EXTRACT NOW:** bank your haul and go home.
- **PUSH DEEPER:** +40% cargo, +25 [[Heat]], an angrier Warden; extract at a different beacon later. Can lead to [[Kelp Cathedral]].

Part of the [[Core Loop]]. The Nest Beacon in [[Eggfall Town]] is one too.
