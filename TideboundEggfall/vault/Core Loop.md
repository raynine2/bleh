# Core Loop

1. **Start in town.** [[Eggfall Town]] is the safe spawn. Talk to [[Gate Warden Sel]] to dive Solo (x1.15 Pearls) or with a crew ([[Crews and Contested Waters]]).
2. **Dive.** Out in the [[Sunken Shoals]]: cast at spots or any water ([[Fishing]], [[Fishing Overhaul]]), swim or ride your fish ([[Swimming and Riding]]), steal eggs, fight [[Mobs]] with your [[Rod Combo]], [[Rod Abilities]] and [[Dashes]].
3. **Heat rises** with every fish and steal ([[Heat]]). The Reef Warden and worse wake up as it climbs.
4. **Extract.** Hold at a beacon, then choose EXTRACT NOW or PUSH DEEPER ([[Extraction and Greed]]). PUSH DEEPER can drop you into [[Kelp Cathedral]].
5. **Spend it.** Hatch [[Tide Eggs]], open [[Dash Capsules]], buy [[Rods]], upgrade your [[Home Nest]] ([[Economy]]).
