# Pity

Guaranteed pulls (`Config.Pity`). A counter goes up with every pull and resets when you get that tier or better.

| Counter | Guaranteed by pull | Gives |
|---|---|---|
| Epic+ | 25 | Epic or better |
| Legendary+ | 90 | Legendary or better |
| Mythic+ | 300 | Mythic or better |
| Celestial (hard pity) | 1200 | Celestial or better |

Each gacha keeps its **own** counters, saved in `data.pity`:
- `TideEgg` for [[Tide Eggs]]
- `DashCapsule` for [[Dash Capsules]]
- `TideChest` for the [[Tide Chest]]

Pity never resets on a wipe ([[Sister Coralie]] says so). See [[Rarity Tiers]].
