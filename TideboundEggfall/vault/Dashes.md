# Dashes

**Q** (B on a gamepad, DASH on touch) rolls one of your equipped dash moves (Patch 19), never the same one twice in a row when you have more than one. Get more from [[Dash Capsules]] and the [[Tide Chest]]; equip up to 3 in the DASHES tab ([[Menu Tabs]]).

| # | Dash | Rarity | Stamina | Air | Needs | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Zoomies | Common | 20 | yes |  | Lean way forward, arms flung back, legs a blur. The starter dash. |
| 2 | Penguin Slide | Uncommon | 22 |  |  | A long belly slide, arms out in front. |
| 3 | Wheel Legs | Common | 20 |  |  | Cartoon wheel legs and windmilling arms. |
| 4 | Barrel Roll | Uncommon | 20 | yes |  | A tucked sideways roll. |
| 5 | Cannonball | Rare | 24 |  |  | A hop into a tucked ball that flips twice through the air, then a ta-da landing. |
| 6 | Pole Vault | Legendary | 26 |  | rod | Plant the rod, backflip up and over it, superhero landing. |
| 7 | Hook Shot | Epic | 26 | yes | rod | Cast the line at a spot ahead, hook it, get yanked along the line. |
| 8 | Rod Copter | Mythic | 28 | yes | rod | Spin the rod over your head like a rotor and glide, legs kicking. |
| 9 | Fish Surf | Epic | 24 |  | companion | Your companion slides under your feet and you surf it. |
| 10 | Fish Rocket | Legendary | 26 | yes | companion | Your companion rams you from behind and you fly off like a superhero. |
| 11 | Tail Slap | Rare | 22 |  | companion | Your companion tail-slaps you into a somersault; you end up sitting, dizzy. |
| 12 | Tidal Warp | Celestial | 30 | yes |  | You dissolve into a rushing wave of light and burst back out further on. |

- **Rod dashes** need your rod (everyone has one). **Companion dashes** need a [[Companions|companion]] out. In the air only air dashes roll.
- Costs [[Stamina and Sprint|stamina]]. Rarity uses [[Rarity Tiers]].
- **Code:** `DashMoves.luau` (data and poses), `TideboundDash` (movement and effects).
