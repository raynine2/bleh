# Harbor Ring

A ring town round a swimmable lagoon south of town, reached by the Tide Slide (`ExpansionBuilder.luau`): snowy studded ground, a stone lagoon rim with gold caps, lamps, trees and plank paths out to every [[Home Nest]].

- **Lagoon Pier:** safe fishing, no [[Heat]], half Pearls, banked at once.
- **Stations:** Daily Quest Board ([[Quill]]), Season Track ([[Captain Brask]]), Leaderboard Hall ([[Archivist Ophel]]), Bait Shop ([[Nim]]), Trading Post ([[Tradesman Kobb]]), Social Plaza with an emote stage and photo spot.
