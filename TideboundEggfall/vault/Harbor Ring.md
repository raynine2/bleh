# Harbor Ring

A ring town round a swimmable lagoon south of town, reached by the Tide Slide (`ExpansionBuilder.luau`). Patch 23: an orange brick promenade round the lagoon with HARBOR spelled out where you arrive, brick paths to every [[Home Nest]], cube trees, bushes and mushrooms.

- **Lagoon Pier:** safe fishing, no [[Heat]], half Pearls, banked at once.
- **Stations:** Daily Quest Board ([[Quill]]), Season Track ([[Captain Brask]]), Leaderboard Hall ([[Archivist Ophel]]), Bait Shop ([[Nim]]), Trading Post ([[Tradesman Kobb]]), Social Plaza with an emote stage and photo spot.
