# Sunken Shoals

Biome 1 (`ShoalsBuilder.luau`, Patch 10): 22 islands in three rings east of [[Eggfall Town]], each with a floating name sign. Bridges, geyser jump pads (`TideboundTravel`), Tide Caches, beacons, wild eggs, the Reef Warden's rock and the Maelstrom.

Where the [[Core Loop]] happens: [[Fishing]], [[Mobs]], [[Heat]], [[Extraction and Greed]]. PUSH DEEPER leads to [[Kelp Cathedral]].
